Why choose ycsts over other CSS frameworks? ▾
ycsts combines the granular utility coverage of Tailwind with the out-of-the-box component convenience of Bootstrap, plus signature single-word entrance animations and zero JavaScript dependencies.
How do entrance animations work? ▾
Simply append fadein, fadein-up, fadein-bounce, or fadein-blur to any element to animate it immediately when rendered.
Pro Tip: Compose animation classes with delay modifiers such as fadein-delay-2 to create staggered cascade entrances.
